About Me

I'm a Computer Science student who is equally curious about what happens beyond the code — how ideas turn into products, how businesses make decisions, and how technology can solve everyday problems.

Alongside my engineering degree at Sri Venkateswara College of Engineering, I'm studying Digital Business and Entrepreneurship at IIM Bangalore. I really enjoy research, working through ideas with others, and exploring where different fields intersect.

I've always felt that design is a part of everyday life rather than something limited to art. I enjoy bringing a little creativity and attention to detail into whatever I work on, and I think that perspective naturally finds its way into both my academic projects and personal pursuits.

Outside academics, you'll usually find me singing, playing the violin, working with acrylic paints, reading, or trying out new craft ideas.

Research & Technical Projects

Peer-Reviewed Research Publication

“Byzantine-Resilient Federated Anomaly Detection for Cyber-Physical Critical Infrastructure Protection”

Role: Co-Author (Sri Varsha, Yaathra P, Vippin Antony) Mentor: Ms. P. Uma, Asst. Professor/CSE, SVCE Journal: IJCRT (Vol. 14, Issue 7, July 2026) ISSN: 2320-2882
  • Core Research Focus: Investigates federated anomaly detection and Byzantine-resilient approaches for distributed cyber-physical critical infrastructure, such as industrial control systems and SCADA networks.
  • Architectural Contribution: Explores the SENTINEL four-layer framework employing Krum aggregation to defend against gradient-poisoning attacks without violating data-sovereignty mandates.
  • Conference Presentations: The author team presented the research at SVCE Innovates 2026 and SVCE ICICRCET.
View DOI Publication ↗ DOI: 10.56975/ijcrt.v14i7.309000
Hackathon Project • Team Build

VERITY — Enterprise Web & Download Security Protection Suite

Forum: FINBEHAVIOUR Hackathon Status: Qualified for Next Round Role: Collaborative Team Member
  • Project Overview: A collaborative browser security solution developed as a Google Chrome Extension to safeguard users from malicious web vectors in real time.
  • Threat Prevention: Designed to identify phishing domains, suspicious redirects, fraudulent payment pages, and potentially harmful executable downloads.
